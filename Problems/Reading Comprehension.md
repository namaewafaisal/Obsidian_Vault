## 1. What Reading Comprehension REALLY tests

RC does **NOT** test:
- vocabulary depth
- opinion
- speed reading

RC tests:
- ability to **locate information**
- ability to **eliminate wrong options**
- ability to **avoid assumptions**

> RC is an elimination game, not a reading test.

---

## 2. Correct way to read the passage (VERY IMPORTANT)

❌ Do NOT read word by word  
❌ Do NOT try to understand everything  

### Correct method (2-pass)

### Pass 1: Skim (60–90 seconds)
Focus only on:
- Topic of passage
- Author’s tone (neutral / critical / supportive)
- Paragraph purpose (intro, example, conclusion)

Ignore:
- examples
- data
- names

---

### Pass 2: Question-driven reading
- Read the **question first**
- Go back to the **relevant paragraph**
- Find the **exact line** related to the question

Never answer from memory.

---

## 3. Common RC Question Types (Exam-focused)

### 1️⃣ Fact-based
- Answer is **directly stated**
- Usually easiest

👉 Strategy: locate line → paraphrase

---

### 2️⃣ Inference-based
- Answer is **implied**, not stated
- Still must be supported by passage

👉 Rule:
If it is **not logically forced**, reject it.

---

### 3️⃣ Main idea / Title
- Overall theme of passage

👉 Reject options that are:
- too narrow
- too extreme
- only about one paragraph

---

### 4️⃣ Tone / Author’s attitude
Words to watch:
- critical
- neutral
- analytical
- supportive

👉 Extreme emotions = usually wrong

---

## 4. Golden Elimination Rules (MOST IMPORTANT)

Use these to **reject options fast**:

❌ Extreme words:
- always
- never
- completely
- only
- must

❌ Outside knowledge  
❌ Assumptions  
❌ Half-true statements  
❌ Correct idea but **wrong scope**

> In RC, a 70% correct option is WRONG.

---

## 5. Time Management Rule (Non-negotiable)

- One RC passage: **6–8 minutes max**
- If stuck between 2 options:
  - choose the **less extreme**
  - move on

Never sink time here.

---

## 6. Mistakes YOU are likely to make (based on your profile)

- Overthinking inference questions
- Bringing logical reasoning outside the passage
- Spending too long validating one option

### Fix:
> The passage is the only truth.  
> Your logic is secondary.

---

## 7. One-line Mental Model (Recall)

> “The correct RC answer is the one that the passage cannot deny.”

---

## 8. Practice Rule

- Practice RC **daily**
- Even 1 passage/day is enough
- Accuracy > speed first

---

## 9. Exam Hall Checklist (RC)

Before locking an answer:
- Can I point to a line in the passage?
- Is this option too strong?
- Is this adding new information?

If any answer is “yes” → reject option.
