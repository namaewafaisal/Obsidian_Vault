## 10. Hard RC Decision Framework (IMPORTANT)

Hard RCs fail students not due to English,
but due to **decision discipline**.

---

## Step 1: Rank answers by SUPPORT (not logic)

When two options both seem reasonable, ask:

1️⃣ Is this **directly supported** by a sentence or conclusion?  
2️⃣ Is this only **logically possible**, but not emphasized?

👉 Always choose **strongly supported** over **merely logical**.

---

## Step 2: Author Dependency Test

Ask for each option:

> “Would the author NEED this option to defend their argument?”

- If **yes** → strong candidate  
- If **no** → weak

---

## Step 3: Scope Precision Test

Reject options that:
- exaggerate impact
- shift focus (worker → technology / society → individual)
- convert conditions into universals

---

## Step 4: Inference ≠ Guessing

Inference means:
- forced by passage
- unavoidable conclusion

It does **not** mean:
- what *could* be true in real life

---

## Final Hard-RC Rule (Memorize)

> In hard RCs, the correct answer is usually the
> **least ambitious option that the passage fully supports**.
