# 🚗 Time, Speed & Distance

---

## 🔴 CORE FORMULAS

$$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$$
$$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$$
$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

---

## 🔴 UNIT CONVERSION (do this first, always)

$$\text{km/h} \rightarrow \text{m/s}: \times \frac{5}{18}$$
$$\text{m/s} \rightarrow \text{km/h}: \times \frac{18}{5}$$

---

## 🔴 RELATIVE SPEED

| Direction | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| Opposite (towards each other) | v₁ + v₂ |
| Same direction | \|v₁ − v₂\| |

---

## 🔴 TRAINS (high frequency)

| Scenario | Distance |
|----------|---------|
| Train crosses a pole / man | Length of train |
| Train crosses platform/bridge | Length of train + Length of platform |
| Two trains crossing | Sum of both lengths |

$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Relative Speed}}$$

---

## 🟠 AVERAGE SPEED

> Never use arithmetic mean for average speed unless speeds are equal.

### Equal distances:
$$\text{Avg Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x+y}$$

### Unequal distances:
$$\text{Avg Speed} = \frac{d_1 + d_2}{\frac{d_1}{v_1} + \frac{d_2}{v_2}}$$

---

## 🟠 BOATS & STREAMS

Let u = speed in still water, v = speed of stream

| | Formula |
|--|---------|
| Downstream speed | u + v |
| Upstream speed | u − v |
| Still water speed | (Downstream + Upstream) / 2 |
| Stream speed | (Downstream − Upstream) / 2 |

---

## 🟡 RACES

> A beats B by d metres in a race of length L:
$$\frac{\text{Speed of A}}{\text{Speed of B}} = \frac{L}{L - d}$$

> A gives B a start of d metres (B starts d m ahead):
$$\text{A runs L, B runs L − d in same time}$$

---

## 🟡 MEETING PROBLEMS

Two people start from A and B towards each other, distance D apart:
$$\text{Time to meet} = \frac{D}{v_1 + v_2}$$

If going in same direction, one chasing other:
$$\text{Time to catch} = \frac{\text{Initial gap}}{v_1 - v_2}$$

---

## 🟠 SOLVED EXAMPLES

### Example 1 — Train crossing platform
> Train 150m long at 54 km/h. Crosses 250m platform in?

Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s
Distance = 150 + 250 = 400m
Time = 400/15 = **26.67 seconds**

---

### Example 2 — Two trains opposite
> Trains 150m and 200m, speeds 60 and 40 km/h, opposite direction.

Relative speed = 100 km/h = 100 × 5/18 = 250/9 m/s
Total length = 350m
Time = 350 ÷ (250/9) = 350 × 9/250 = **12.6 seconds**

---

### Example 3 — Same direction
> Train 100m at 72 km/h overtakes train 200m at 36 km/h (same direction). Time?

Relative speed = 72 − 36 = 36 km/h = 10 m/s
Total length = 300m
Time = 300/10 = **30 seconds**

---

### Example 4 — Boats
> Boat speed 15 km/h in still water, stream 3 km/h. Time for 36 km upstream + 36 km downstream?

Upstream speed = 15−3 = 12, time = 36/12 = 3 hrs
Downstream speed = 15+3 = 18, time = 36/18 = 2 hrs
Total = **5 hours**

---

### Example 5 — Average speed trap
> Man goes at 40 km/h, returns at 60 km/h. Average speed?

= 2 × 40 × 60 / (40 + 60) = 4800/100 = **48 km/h**
(NOT 50 km/h — never average the speeds)

---

### Example 6 — Late/early problems
> A man covers distance in 3 hrs if speed is 75 km/h. He wants to cover same in 2.5 hrs. Required speed?

Distance = 75 × 3 = 225 km
Speed = 225/2.5 = **90 km/h**

---

## 🟠 SOLVING APPROACH

1. Always convert units first — get everything in same unit
2. For trains — identify if crossing pole/platform/other train → find total distance
3. For relative speed — identify direction first (same/opposite)
4. For boats — memorize which formula gives u and which gives v
5. For average speed — always use harmonic mean formula, never arithmetic

## 🟡 PRACTICE PROBLEMS

**P1.** A train 200m long crosses a bridge 300m long at 72 km/h. Time?

**P2.** Two trains start from stations A and B, 300 km apart, towards each other at 50 and 70 km/h. When do they meet?

**P3.** A man rows 16 km downstream in 2 hrs and 8 km upstream in 2 hrs. Speed of stream?

**P4.** A person travelling at 5/6th of usual speed reaches 10 min late. Usual time?

**P5.** A train passes a pole in 15 seconds and a 200m platform in 25 seconds. Length and speed of train?

**P6.** In a 1km race, A beats B by 100m. B beats C by 100m. By how much does A beat C?

**P7.** A car travels first 100km at 50 km/h, next 100km at 100 km/h. Average speed?

**P8.** A boat goes 30 km upstream in 3 hrs and 30 km downstream in 2 hrs. Speed in still water?

---

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES

- Forgetting to convert km/h to m/s when answer needs to be in seconds
- Using arithmetic mean for average speed
- Wrong total distance for two trains crossing (must add both lengths)
- Upstream = slower speed (subtracting stream), downstream = faster
