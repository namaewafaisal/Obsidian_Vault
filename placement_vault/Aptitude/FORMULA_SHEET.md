# ⚡ APTITUDE FORMULA SHEET

> Print this. Read before exam. All formulas, no explanation.

---

## PERCENTAGE
- x% of Y = (x/100)×Y
- A is what % of B = (A/B)×100
- % change = (change/original)×100
- Successive change = a+b+(ab/100) [use − for decrease]
- Same % up then down → net loss = x²/100

## PROFIT & LOSS
- Profit% = (SP−CP)/CP × 100 [always on CP]
- SP = CP×(100+P)/100 or CP×(100−L)/100
- CP = SP×100/(100+P) or SP×100/(100−L)
- SP after discount = MP×(100−D)/100
- Markup+Discount → SP = CP×(100+M)/100×(100−D)/100
- Two items same SP same % → Net loss = x²/100
- CP of m = SP of n → Profit = (m−n)/n × 100
- Selling x at price of y → Profit = (y−x)/x × 100
- False weight gain = (true−false)/false × 100

## TIME & WORK
- A and B together = xy/(x+y) days
- LCM method: Total work = LCM, rates = LCM/days
- A+B+C = [(A+B)+(B+C)+(A+C)] / 2
- M₁D₁ = M₂D₂ (same work)
- Pipes: net rate = 1/fill − 1/empty

## TIME SPEED DISTANCE
- S = D/T, D = S×T, T = D/S
- km/h → m/s: ×5/18
- m/s → km/h: ×18/5
- Relative speed: opposite = add, same = subtract
- Train cross pole = own length; cross platform = train+platform
- Two trains cross = sum of lengths / relative speed
- Average speed (equal dist) = 2xy/(x+y)
- Downstream = u+v, Upstream = u−v
- Still water = (D+U)/2, Stream = (D−U)/2

## SIMPLE INTEREST
- SI = PRT/100
- Doubles → T = 100/R, Triples → T = 200/R

## COMPOUND INTEREST
- A = P(1+R/100)^T
- Half-yearly: rate/2, time×2
- Quarterly: rate/4, time×4
- CI−SI (2yr) = P(R/100)²
- CI−SI (3yr) = P(R/100)²×(3+R/100)

## RATIO & PROPORTION
- Share = (part/total) × whole
- Combined ratio: make common term, use LCM
- a:b = c:d → ad = bc

## AVERAGES
- Avg = Sum/Count
- New member = old avg + n×change (+ if avg increases)
- Weighted avg = (n₁x₁+n₂x₂)/(n₁+n₂)

## NUMBER SYSTEMS
- Unit digit: find power mod 4 → use cycle [2:2,4,8,6 | 3:3,9,7,1 | 7:7,9,3,1 | 8:8,4,2,6 | 4:4,6 | 9:9,1]
- Zeros in n! = ⌊n/5⌋+⌊n/25⌋+⌊n/125⌋...
- HCF×LCM = product of two numbers
