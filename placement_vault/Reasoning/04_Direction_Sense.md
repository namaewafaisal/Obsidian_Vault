# 🧭 Direction Sense

---

## 🔴 THE COMPASS

```
        North
          ↑
West ←    +    → East
          ↓
        South
```

Diagonals: NE, NW, SE, SW (45° from each axis)

---

## 🔴 TURNS

| From | Turn Right | Turn Left |
|------|-----------|----------|
| North | East | West |
| East | South | North |
| South | West | East |
| West | North | South |

**180° turn** = complete reversal (N→S, E→W, etc.)

**Clockwise rotation:**
N → E → S → W → N

---

## 🔴 DISTANCE (DISPLACEMENT)

For final distance from start: use **Pythagorean theorem** when movements are perpendicular.

If moved a km North/South and b km East/West:
$$\text{Distance from start} = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$$

If only one direction net → straight line distance.

---

## 🟠 SOLVED EXAMPLES

### Example 1 — Net displacement
> Walks 5km N, 3km E, 5km S. Distance and direction from start?

5km N and 5km S cancel. Net = 3km East. Distance = **3km East** from start.

### Example 2 — Pythagoras
> Walks 4km N, 3km E. Distance from start?

= √(4²+3²) = √25 = **5km** (NE direction)

### Example 3 — Turn-based
> Facing East. Turns right. Walks 5km. Turns left. Walks 3km. Turns right. Walks 5km. Final direction?

Facing East → turn right → now facing South.
Walk 5km South.
Turn left → now facing East.
Walk 3km East.
Turn right → now facing South.
Final direction: **South**

### Example 4 — Angle rotation
> Facing SW. Turns 135° clockwise.

Clockwise from SW: SW→W→NW→N (each 45°). 135° = 3 steps.
SW → W → NW → **N**

## 🟡 PRACTICE PROBLEMS

**P1.** A walks 10m N, turns right walks 5m, turns right walks 10m, turns left walks 3m. Distance and direction from start?

**P2.** A person facing North-West turns 90° clockwise. Which direction now?

**P3.** Ram starts from home, walks 3km West, turns right walks 4km North. Distance from home?

**P4.** A goes 6km N, 4km E, 6km S, 4km W. Where is A relative to start?

**P5.** Person faces South. Turns 180°. Turns 45° anticlockwise. Direction now?

---

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES

- Confusing clockwise and anticlockwise
- Not cancelling opposing directions before applying Pythagoras
- Forgetting diagonal directions (NE, SW etc.) when turn is 45°
