# ⚡ REASONING QUICK REFERENCE

---

## SYLLOGISM
- All+All=All | All+No=No | Some+All=Some | Some+No=SomeNot
- All+Some=❌ | Some+Some=❌ | No+No=❌
- Convert: No↔No (reverse OK) | Some↔Some (reverse OK) | All→cannot reverse | SomeNot→cannot reverse
- Complementary pair → Either/Or: (SomeAB + NoAB) or (SomeAB + SomeAnotB)

## BLOOD RELATIONS
- "Only daughter of my mother" = the speaker herself
- "Only son of my grandfather" = my father
- Draw the tree always. Never solve blind.
- Nephew = brother's/sister's son | Niece = brother's/sister's daughter

## SEATING — LINEAR
- Position from right = (N+1) − position from left
- "Immediate right/left" = directly adjacent
- "2nd from left" = 2nd position counting from left end

## SEATING — CIRCULAR
- Facing centre: YOUR left = clockwise, YOUR right = anticlockwise
- Opposite = N/2 seats away
- Draw, place one anchor, build around it

## DIRECTION SENSE
- Clockwise turns: N→E→S→W→N
- Right turn = 90° clockwise | Left turn = 90° anticlockwise
- Net displacement: cancel opposing directions, Pythagoras for perpendicular
- 45° turn = diagonal direction (NE, NW, SE, SW)

## CODING-DECODING
- Find shift: compare letter positions
- Verify shift on ALL examples
- For number codes: find common digit in overlapping sentences
- For sum codes: sum = sum of A-Z position values

## SERIES — CHECK IN ORDER
1. Constant difference (AP)?
2. Increasing difference?
3. Multiplication factor (GP)?
4. Squares/Cubes/n(n+1)?
5. Alternating series (split odd/even terms)?
6. Previous two terms added?

## STATEMENT-CONCLUSION
- "All" and "never" in conclusions → risky, check strictly
- Inverse of statement → usually NOT valid
- Assumption = something implied but not stated, necessary for statement to hold
- Course of action = must be practical and directly address the problem

## INEQUALITIES
- Chain connects → can conclude
- Gap in chain → cannot conclude
- A>B>C → A>C ✅ | A>B and D<B → A vs D = ❌ (gap)
- Always check if relation is strict (>) or can be equal (≥)
