# 📖 Reading Comprehension (RC)

---

## 🔴 THE CORE STRATEGY (do this every time)

1. **Read the questions first** — know what to look for before reading
2. **Skim the passage** — first and last sentence of each paragraph
3. **Answer from the passage only** — never use outside knowledge
4. **Eliminate wrong options** — 3 wrong is easier than finding 1 right
5. **Inference questions** — must be supported by passage, not just "possible"

---

## 🔴 QUESTION TYPES AND HOW TO ANSWER EACH

### Type 1 — Direct/Factual
*"According to the passage, what was the effect of X?"*
- Answer is directly stated in the passage
- Find the exact line, match the option
- Don't overthink

### Type 2 — Vocabulary in Context
*"The word 'X' in the passage most closely means?"*
- Read the full sentence where the word appears
- Substitute each option — which fits the meaning of the sentence?
- Don't pick the dictionary meaning blindly

### Type 3 — Main Idea / Central Theme
*"The passage is primarily about?"*
- Must cover the WHOLE passage, not just one paragraph
- Eliminate options that are too narrow (one paragraph only)
- Eliminate options that are too broad (not discussed in passage)

### Type 4 — Inference
*"It can be inferred from the passage that...?"*
- Must be implied by the passage, not directly stated
- "Possible" ≠ "Inferable" — must be logically drawn
- Eliminate options that require outside knowledge

### Type 5 — Author's Tone/Purpose
*"The author's tone in this passage is...?"*
- Look at word choices: positive/negative/neutral words
- Common tones: analytical, critical, objective, appreciative, cautionary, satirical

### Type 6 — NOT/EXCEPT questions
*"Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the passage?"*
- Verify all options against the passage
- The correct answer is the one you cannot find support for

---

## 🔴 ELIMINATION RULES

Eliminate options that are:
- **Too extreme**: "always", "never", "only", "all" — very rarely correct
- **Too narrow**: covers only one small part of a big passage
- **Too broad**: goes beyond what the passage says
- **Opposite**: contradicts the passage
- **Out of scope**: not discussed in the passage at all

## 🟠 PASSAGE + SOLVED QUESTIONS

### Passage 1 — History
*"The Industrial Revolution, beginning in Britain in the late 18th century, transformed human society. Prior to this, most people lived rurally and worked in agriculture. Steam-powered machinery enabled mass production in factories, drawing workers into expanding urban centers. While this brought economic growth, it also created dire working conditions — long hours, child labor, dangerous environments. Social reformers and labor movements eventually led to legislation improving workers' rights."*

**Q1. What was the primary effect of steam-powered machinery?**
Answer: Enabled mass production in factories / drew workers into urban centers.
(Direct — stated in line 3)

**Q2. Which is NOT mentioned as a negative effect?**
Options: a) Long hours b) Child labor c) Environmental pollution d) Dangerous workplaces
Answer: **c) Environmental pollution** — not mentioned in the passage.

**Q3. 'Dire' most closely means?**
Context: "dire working conditions"
a) Good b) Severe/terrible c) Modern d) Expensive
Answer: **b) Severe** — dire = extremely serious/terrible.

**Q4. What can be inferred about pre-Industrial Revolution life?**
Answer: Most people were farmers/rural workers (directly supported by passage).
Cannot infer: "People were happy" — not supported.

**Q5. Workers' rights improved because of?**
Answer: Social reformers and early labor movements pushed for legislation.

---

### Passage 2 — Science
*"Antibiotics have been one of the most important medical discoveries of the 20th century, saving millions of lives. However, their overuse and misuse have led to antibiotic resistance — a growing global health crisis. When bacteria are repeatedly exposed to antibiotics, they can evolve mechanisms to survive the treatment. This makes common infections harder to treat and increases the risk of severe illness and death. The World Health Organization has declared antibiotic resistance one of the biggest threats to global health, urging nations to use antibiotics only when prescribed and to develop new drugs."*

**Q1. What causes antibiotic resistance?**
Answer: Overuse and misuse of antibiotics — bacteria evolve to survive.

**Q2. The tone of this passage is best described as?**
a) Celebratory b) Cautionary c) Humorous d) Indifferent
Answer: **b) Cautionary** — warns about a growing threat.

**Q3. 'Urging' most closely means?**
a) Forcing b) Requesting c) Strongly encouraging d) Preventing
Answer: **c) Strongly encouraging**

**Q4. What can be inferred?**
a) All antibiotics should be banned.
b) New drug development is needed.
c) Bacteria were harmless before antibiotics.
d) The WHO invented antibiotics.
Answer: **b) New drug development is needed** — directly stated + supported.

## 🟠 APPROACH FOR LONG PASSAGES (400+ words)

1. Read Q first — note line numbers if options point to specific parts
2. First sentence of each paragraph = topic sentence (most important)
3. Last paragraph = conclusion/solution/author's recommendation
4. Time yourself: 2 min to read, 1 min per question max

## 🟡 PRACTICE PASSAGE

*"Sleep is often the first casualty of our busy modern lives, yet its importance cannot be overstated. During sleep, the brain consolidates memories, repairs cells, and regulates hormones essential for growth and stress management. Chronic sleep deprivation has been linked to a host of health problems, including obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and impaired cognitive function. Despite this, surveys show that nearly one-third of adults in developed countries get less than the recommended seven hours per night. The solution lies not in expensive interventions but in behavioral changes — maintaining a consistent sleep schedule, reducing screen exposure before bedtime, and creating a calm sleep environment."*

**P1.** What does the brain do during sleep? (List 3 things)

**P2.** The word 'consolidated' (if it appeared) would most likely mean?
a) Destroyed b) Strengthened/organized c) Forgotten d) Created

**P3.** Which is NOT mentioned as a consequence of sleep deprivation?
a) Obesity b) Diabetes c) Depression d) Cardiovascular disease

**P4.** What is the author's main suggestion?

**P5.** The tone of this passage is:
a) Alarming and pessimistic b) Informative and advisory c) Humorous d) Celebratory

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES

- Reading the full passage before questions → waste of time
- Picking options that "sound correct" without verifying in passage
- Confusing "inferred" with "possible" — inference must be logically drawn
- Vocabulary questions: using dictionary meaning instead of context meaning
- NOT questions: spending too little time — verify each option
