# Synonyms

### 1. CORPULENT

❓ **Question Word:** **Corpulent** — Having a large, bulky body; obese.

* **Lean** — Thin and healthy with very little body fat.
* **Gaunt** — Extremely thin and bony, usually from sickness or hunger.
* **Emaciated** — Abnormally thin and weak due to illness or a lack of food.
* ✅ **Obese** — Grossly fat or overweight in a way that affects health.

---

### 2. BRIEF

❓ **Question Word:** **Brief** — Lasting for a short period of time or using few words.

* **Limited** — Restricted in size, amount, or extent.
* **Small** — Low in physical size, scale, or capability.
* **Little** — Small in size, amount, degree, or age.
* ✅ **Short** — Measuring a small distance or lasting a brief time.

---

### 3. EMBEZZLE

❓ **Question Word:** **Embezzle** — To secretly steal funds placed in one's trust or care.

* ✅ **Misappropriate** — To dishonestly take something, especially money, for one's own use.
* **Balance** — To keep something in a steady position or even proportions.
* **Remunerate** — To pay someone for services rendered or work done.
* **Clear** — Free from darkness, confusion, obstacles, or guilt.

---

### 4. VENT

❓ **Question Word:** **Vent** — An opening that allows air, gas, or liquid to escape.

* ✅ **Opening** — A gap or space that allows access or passage through.
* **Stodge** — Heavy, thick, and filling food (often high in carbohydrates).
* **End** — The final part, finish, or termination of something.
* **Past tense of go** — The word "went," which is phonetically similar but different.

---

### 5. AUGUST

❓ **Question Word:** **August** — Inspiring respect, dignity, and admiration; grand.

* **Common** — Occurring frequently, ordinary, or shared by many.
* **Ridiculous** — Deserving or inviting mockery; completely absurd.
* ✅ **Dignified** — Showing a serious, respectful, and noble manner.
* **Petty** — Of little importance, trivial, or small-minded.
