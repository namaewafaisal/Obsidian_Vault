# 🧠 OO Methodologies Suitability

## 🧩 Core Idea
Different OO methodologies are suited for different phases of software development.

---

## ⚙️ Mapping

### 🔹 Design
- **Booch Method**
- Strong in object-level design  
- Handles complex structures  

---

### 🔹 Analysis
- **OOSE (Jacobson)**
- Focus on use cases  
- Strong in requirement analysis  

---

### 🔹 Full Life Cycle
- **OMT (Rumbaugh)**
- Covers analysis, design, implementation  

---

### 🔹 Real-Time Systems
- **Booch Method**
- Suitable for dynamic and complex systems  

---

## ⚖️ Summary Table

| Phase             | Method        |
|------------------|--------------|
| Design           | Booch        |
| Analysis         | OOSE         |
| Full Life Cycle  | OMT          |
| Real-Time        | Booch        |

---

## 🧠 Final Understanding

- Booch → **Design & complex systems**  
- OOSE → **Analysis (use cases)**  
- OMT → **Complete lifecycle**
