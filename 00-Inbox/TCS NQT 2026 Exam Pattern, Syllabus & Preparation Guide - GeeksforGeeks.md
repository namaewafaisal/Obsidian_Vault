## TCS NQT Eligibility Criteria

TCS maintains strict academic hygiene. Any discrepancy in these details usually leads to disqualification during document verification.

-   ****Degrees Allowed****: B.E. / B.Tech / M.E. / M.Tech / MCA / M.Sc / M.S.
-   ****Percentage Criteria****: Minimum 60% or 6.0 CGPA throughout (10th, 12th, Diploma, UG, and PG).
-   ****Backlogs****: Maximum 1 active backlog allowed at the time of the test. All backlogs must be cleared before joining.
-   ****Academic Gap****: Overall academic gap must not exceed 24 months (2 years). Gaps must be declared with valid reasons.
-   ****Age Limit****: Minimum 18 years and Maximum 28 years.
-   ****Course Type****: Only Full-Time courses are considered. (NIOS is acceptable for 10th/12th only).

## TCS NQT Selection Process

After the online test, candidates are "tagged" to a specific role based on their percentile. Each role has a different interview difficulty level.

### 1\. Online Test

Stage 1 is the online test. The examination comprises two sections and these are conducted at designated TCS iON centers. Under the new integrated pattern, both sections are mandatory to be considered for the higher-tier Digital and Prime roles.

### 2\. Interview Stages

If you clear the NQT cutoff, you will be invited for a face-to-face or virtual interview panel consisting of:

-   ****Technical Round****: Deep dive into Data Structures, OOPS, DBMS, and your final year project.
-   ****Managerial Round****: Focuses on problem-solving, situational judgment, and project management skills.
-   ****HR Round****: Behavioral assessment, communication check, and verification of documents/relocation flexibility.

## Important Exam Day Guidelines

-   ****In-Centre Only****: The majority of NQTs are now conducted at TCS iON Authorized Centers.
-   ****Question Locking****: Once you move to the next question, you cannot go back to change the previous answer.
-   ****Rough Work****: Physical paper is generally provided at the center and submitted before leaving the center.
-   ****No Tab Switching****: Any attempt to switch tabs or windows will terminate the exam immediately.
