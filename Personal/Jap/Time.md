# ⏰ Japanese — Time & Daily Expressions

These words appear **every single day** in conversations, anime, and real life.

---

### 1. 今  
いま  
*ima*  
now  

今する  
*ima suru*  
I’ll do it now.

---

### 2. 今日  
きょう  
*kyō*  
today  

今日行く  
*kyō iku*  
I’m going today.

---

### 3. 明日  
あした  
*ashita*  
tomorrow  

明日来る  
*ashita kuru*  
I’ll come tomorrow.

---

### 4. 昨日  
きのう  
*kinō*  
yesterday  

昨日した  
*kinō shita*  
I did it yesterday.

---

### 5. もう  
もう  
*mō*  
already  

もう食べた  
*mō tabeta*  
Already ate.

---

### 6. まだ  
まだ  
*mada*  
not yet / still  

まだする  
*mada suru*  
I’ll still do it / Not yet done.

---

### 7. すぐ  
すぐ  
*sugu*  
soon / immediately  

すぐ行く  
*sugu iku*  
I’ll go right away.

---

### 8. いつ  
いつ  
*itsu*  
when  

いつ行く？  
*itsu iku?*  
When are you going?

---

### 9. 毎日  
まいにち  
*mainichi*  
every day  

毎日見る  
*mainichi miru*  
I watch every day.

---

### 10. 時間  
じかん  
*jikan*  
time  

時間ある  
*jikan aru*  
I have time.

---

## ✅ How to use this today

- Read **each word aloud**
- Read **each example aloud**
- No memorization
- No grammar analysis
- Stop when time is up
