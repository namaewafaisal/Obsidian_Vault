# 🇯🇵 Japanese Learning Plan (1 Hour / Week)

> **Principle:** Consistency beats intensity.  
> 10 minutes × 6 days is more effective than one 60-minute session.

This plan is designed for **extreme time constraints** while ensuring **correct long-term foundations**.

---

## ⏱ Weekly Structure

- **Total time:** ~60 minutes/week  
- **Daily sessions:** 10 minutes × 6 days  
- **Goal:** Continuous exposure, not speed

---

## 📘 Phase 1: Phonetic Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

**Objective:** Full script literacy (Hiragana → Katakana)

### Days 1–4 — Script Drilling (10 min/day)
- Learn **[^1]Hiragana**
- Use **mnemonics** (shape → sound association)
- ❌ Do NOT use romaji
- Focus on **recognition**, not handwriting

### Day 5 — Pronunciation Basics (10 min)
- Master the 5 vowel sounds:
  - あ (a), い (i), う (u), え (e), お (o)
- Speak **flat and clear**
- Be aware that **pitch accent exists**, but:
  - ❌ Do NOT study pitch patterns yet

### Day 6 — Typing Practice (10 min)
- Install a **Japanese IME**
- Practice typing Hiragana
- Prioritize **digital input** over handwriting

---

## 📗 Phase 2: Core Language Building (Weeks 5–12)

**Objective:** Basic sentence construction + high-frequency vocabulary

### Days 1–3 — Sentence Structure (SOV)
- Internalize **Subject – Object – Verb**
- The **verb always comes last**

**Example:**
```
I eat sushi
→ I sushi eat
→ わたしは すしを たべます
```

---

### Days 4–5 — Essential Particles

Learn **one particle at a time**.

- **は (wa):** Topic marker  
- **を (o):** Direct object  
- **に (ni):** Destination / specific time  

> Particles define meaning more than word order.

---

### Day 6 — Vocabulary Review (SRS)

- Use **Anki / LingoDeer**
- Add **max 5 new words/day**
- Focus on **high-frequency vocabulary (80/20 rule)**

**Rule:**
- If reviews exceed 5 minutes → stop adding new words

---

## 🛠 Recommended Tools (Minimal Stack)

Choose **one primary tool**.

1. **LingoDeer**
   - Best grammar + audio for Japanese
2. **JapanesePod101**
   - 10-minute listening lessons
3. **Bunpro**
   - Grammar SRS (use only after basics)

❌ Do NOT use all tools at once.

---

## ⚠️ Rules That Must Not Be Broken

- ❌ Do not skip more than **2 consecutive days**
- If busy:
  - Read 1 phrase
  - Say 1 sentence aloud
  - Even 2 minutes is enough

---

## 🎯 Expected Outcome (After ~3 Months)

- Full kana literacy
- Basic sentence parsing
- Familiarity with particles
- Early listening comprehension
- ❌ No fluency (this is normal)

---

## 🧠 Learning Metaphor

Learning Japanese with 1 hour/week is like **laying stones across a garden**:

- One big stone per week → easy to forget
- One small stone per day → a stable path forms

Consistency builds a path you can walk on later.

You *can* build **recognition vocabulary** without mastering scripts **initially**.

## 2. The revised system (glossary-only, minimal)

### Core rule

---

## 3. Daily glossary structure (this is non-negotiable)

Each entry must contain **all three layers**:

```
日本語
romaji
English meaning
```

Optionally:

* Short usage note
* One simple example

### Example glossary entry

```
赤い
akai
red

Used to describe colour of objects.
Example: 赤い車 (akai kuruma) – red car
```

No grammar explanations. No theory.

---

## 4. What words to include (important filter)

Only include **high-utility words**:

### Good candidates

* Colours
* Family terms
* Common verbs (go, eat, see, do)
* Everyday nouns (car, food, shop)
* Adjectives used in daily speech

### Avoid for now

* Abstract words
* JLPT lists
* Rare kanji-only terms
* Synonym clusters

---

## 5. How many words per day (hard limit)

* **5–7 words per day**
* Never more

Why:

* Reading > memorizing
* You are building **familiarity**, not recall precision

---

## 6. How to “study” the glossary (10 minutes)

1. Read today’s glossary **out loud**
2. Read yesterday’s glossary **once**
3. Stop

No drills. No testing. No rewriting.
