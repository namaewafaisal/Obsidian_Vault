## AI Tools for Generating Anki Flashcards

### **1. AnkiDecks**

- **What it does:** Upload PDFs, slides, Word docs, YouTube links, and more. It can generate Question-Answer, Cloze (fill-in-the-blank), Multiple Choice, and Image Occlusion flashcards. You can export decks to Anki or use its built-in spaced repetition study tool. [anki-decks.com+1](https://anki-decks.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **2. Ankify**

- **What it does:** Lets you upload notes, slides, PDFs, or paste text. AI generates flashcards that you can preview, edit, then export as an `.apkg` file for Anki. [Ankify+1](https://www.ankify.app/blog/anki-card-generator?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **3. AnkiAI**

- **What it does:** Upload a PDF and get perfectly formatted Anki flashcards in around 60 seconds. [Anki AI](https://www.ankiai.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **4. NovaCards**

- **What it does:** Offers an Anki plugin that lets you generate cards directly within Anki. It also helps you find relevant cards from popular decks like AnKing and MangoMedic. [novacards.ai+1](https://novacards.ai/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **5. StudyCards AI**

- **What it does:** Upload PDFs and let AI generate normal and cloze flashcards, then export them to Anki. Particularly popular among medical students. [StudyCards AI](https://studycardsai.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **6. AI 4 ANKI**

- **What it does:** Tailored for language learners—generates sentence decks with audio in various languages. Downloadable and directly importable into Anki. [ai4anki.com](https://www.ai4anki.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **7. Jungle AI**

- **What it does:** Upload notes, textbooks, slides, webpages, or even YouTube videos, then generate Anki-ready flashcards and export them. [jungleai.com](https://jungleai.com/use-cases/create-anki-flashcards?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **8. Ai-Anki-Generator (open-source)**

- **What it does:** A free, open-source tool that takes your text and outputs question-answer pairs formatted for Anki. Requires setting up via Python or a ready-to-use build. [AnkiHub Community](https://community.ankihub.net/t/ai-anki-deck-generator-fully-automated-deck-creation-using-ai/375851?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

### **9. Anki Card Generator (GPT-4-based)**

- **What it does:** A simpler online tool where you enter a topic and GPT-4 generates relevant Anki-style flashcard
