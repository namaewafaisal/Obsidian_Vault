## Service agreement / Relocation / Shifts
Usually straightforward yes/no — TCS roles commonly involve a bond period and pan-India posting. Decide honestly beforehand what's actually acceptable; don't answer reflexively since this gets formalized later.

> "I'm genuinely into systems-level tinkering — I run Arch Linux as my daily driver, manage my own dotfiles, and I've gone through major setup migrations just to understand how the pieces fit together. It's less a hobby and more a continuous learning habit."

## Career Goals / Where in 5 years
> "In the near term, I want to grow as a backend/systems engineer, deepen my understanding of large-scale architecture, and contribute meaningfully to real projects. Longer term, I'm drawn to systems-level and platform engineering work — but I see this role as the right place to build that foundation."

## Is a LinkedIn/social profile mandatory for career growth?
Opinion question — testing reasoning, not recitation. Balanced take: not *mandatory*, but helps visibility/networking/recruiter discovery. For a backend role, GitHub arguably matters more — it's a direct demonstration of work rather than self-presentation.

## Questions to ask them
Always have 1-2 ready:
- What kind of projects do fresh Prime hires typically get placed into?
- What does the learning & development path look like for someone wanting to grow into more systems-heavy work?
