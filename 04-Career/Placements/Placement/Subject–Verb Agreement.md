## 1. What Subject–Verb Agreement really means

The **verb must agree with the SUBJECT**,  
**not** with:
- nearby nouns
- objects
- phrases in between

> Find the real subject → match the verb to it.

---

## 2. Core Rule (80% questions)

### Singular subject → singular verb  
### Plural subject → plural verb

Examples:
- The **student** *is* intelligent.
- The **students** *are* intelligent.

Sounds obvious — exam questions hide the subject.

---

## 3. The MOST COMMON TRAP (Very important)

### Subject + prepositional phrase

Ignore phrases starting with:
- of
- with
- along with
- together with
- as well as

Examples:
- The **quality** of the apples *is* good. ✅  
- The **teacher**, along with his students, *is* attending. ✅  

👉 Subject = **quality**, **teacher**  
👉 NOT apples / students

---

## 4. Either / Neither / Each / Everyone / Someone

These are **ALWAYS singular**.

Examples:
- **Each** of the players *is* ready.
- **Everyone** *has* arrived.
- **Neither** of the answers *is* correct.

❌ Common mistake: treating “of the + plural noun” as plural

---

## 5. Either–Or / Neither–Nor Rule

The verb agrees with the **nearest subject**.

Examples:
- Either the **teacher** or the **students are** responsible. ✅  
- Either the **students** or the **teacher is** responsible. ✅  

👉 Look **closest to the verb**

---

## 6. Collective Nouns (exam-safe rule)

Words like:
- team
- committee
- family
- government

### Exam rule:
- If acting as **one unit** → singular
- If acting as **individuals** → plural

Examples:
- The team *is* winning. ✅  
- The team *are* arguing among themselves. ✅  

If unclear → **singular is safer** in aptitude exams.

---

## 7. Uncountable Nouns (always singular)

Examples:
- information
- furniture
- advice
- equipment
- luggage
- news

Examples:
- The information *is* useful. ✅  
- The news *is* shocking. ✅  

❌ “informations”, “furnitures” → WRONG

---

## 8. Titles, Subjects, Distances, Amounts

Even if plural in form → treated as **singular**.

Examples:
- Mathematics *is* difficult.
- Ten kilometers *is* a long distance.
- Five years *is* enough.

---

## 9. “The number of” vs “A number of” (FREQUENT)

- **The number of** → singular  
- **A number of** → plural

Examples:
- The number of students *is* increasing. ✅  
- A number of students *are* absent. ✅  

---

## 10. How to solve SVA questions in exam (Step method)

1. Identify the **main verb**
2. Ask: *who is doing the action?*
3. Ignore everything after **of / with / along with**
4. Match verb to **real subject**
5. Eliminate options that “sound right” but break rules

---

### Fix:
> Grammar ≠ logic  
> Grammar = rule application

---

## 12. One-line recall (VERY IMPORTANT)

> “The verb agrees with the subject — not with the nearest noun.”
