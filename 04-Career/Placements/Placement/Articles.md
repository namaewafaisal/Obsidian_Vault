## 1. What articles indicate

Articles show:
- whether a noun is **specific**
- or **general**

---

## 2. Indefinite Articles — a / an

Used when:
- noun is mentioned **first time**
- noun is **not specific**

### a → consonant sound  
### an → vowel sound (sound, not letter)

Examples:
- a university ✅
- an hour ✅

---

## 3. Definite Article — the

Used when:
- noun is **specific**
- mentioned earlier
- unique (sun, moon)
- superlatives

Examples:
- I saw a movie. The movie was good.
- the best student

---

## 4. Zero Article (NO article)

Used before:
- plural nouns (general)
- abstract nouns
- uncountable nouns

Examples:
- Students are hardworking.
- Honesty is important.
- Information is useful.

---

## 5. MOST COMMON EXAM TRAPS

❌ the + plural (general meaning)  
❌ a/an before uncountable nouns  
❌ missing article before singular countable noun  

Wrong:
- He bought **car** ❌  
Correct:
- He bought **a car** ✅

---

## 6. One-line recall

> “Use ‘a/an’ for general, ‘the’ for specific, and nothing for abstract/general plural.”
