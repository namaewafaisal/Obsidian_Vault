# ✏️ Grammar & Error Spotting

---

## 🔴 SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT (most tested)

### Rule 1 — Singular subjects take singular verbs
> The boy **runs** fast. ✅
> The boy **run** fast. ❌

### Rule 2 — "Each", "Every", "Either", "Neither", "None" → singular verb
> Each of the students **is** present. ✅
> Each of the students **are** present. ❌
> Neither of them **is** ready. ✅

### Rule 3 — Collective nouns → usually singular
> The committee **has** decided. ✅
> The team **is** playing well. ✅

### Rule 4 — "A number of" → plural | "The number of" → singular
> A number of students **were** absent. ✅
> The number of students **is** increasing. ✅

### Rule 5 — "One of the X who/that" → verb agrees with X (plural)
> He is one of the boys who **have** always been punctual. ✅
> (boys = plural → have, not has)

### Rule 6 — Compound subject with "and" → plural verb
> Ram and Shyam **are** coming. ✅
> Exception: "bread and butter", "slow and steady" → singular concept → singular verb

### Rule 7 — "Or/Nor" → verb agrees with nearest subject
> Neither the students nor the teacher **was** present. ✅
> Neither the teacher nor the students **were** present. ✅

### Rule 8 — Data, news, information, furniture, luggage → always singular
> The data **is** incorrect. ✅ (formal/technical)
> The news **is** good. ✅

## 🔴 TENSES (second most tested)

### Rule 1 — Present Perfect: have/has + past participle
> He **has gone** to the market. ✅
> He **has went** to the market. ❌

### Rule 2 — Past Perfect: had + past participle (for earlier of two past events)
> He **had left** before she arrived. ✅

### Rule 3 — Conditional: If + past → would + base verb
> If I **were** you, I would apologize. ✅ (subjunctive)
> If I **was** you... ❌ (informal — avoid in exams)

### Rule 4 — "Since" → present perfect | "Ago" → simple past
> I have been here **since** Monday. ✅
> I came here three days **ago**. ✅
> I have come here three days ago. ❌

### Rule 5 — Two past events: earlier one = past perfect
> She **had finished** her work before he **arrived**. ✅

## 🔴 ARTICLES (a / an / the)

### a vs an
- **a** before consonant sound: a book, a university (yoo sound), a one (won sound)
- **an** before vowel sound: an apple, an hour (silent h), an MBA

### the
- Specific/known thing: **The** book you gave me.
- Unique things: **The** sun, **the** moon, **the** President.
- Superlatives: **The** best student.
- "The same", "the only", "the first"

### No article
- General plural: **Dogs** are loyal. (not "The dogs")
- Languages, sports, meals: She speaks **English**. He plays **cricket**.
- Titles with names: **President Obama** (not "the President Obama")

## 🔴 PRONOUNS

### Rule 1 — Pronoun must agree with antecedent in number
> Each student must submit **his or her** assignment. ✅
> Each student must submit **their** assignment. ✅ (modern usage)

### Rule 2 — Object pronoun after prepositions
> Between you and **me** (not I). ✅
> For **him** and **her** (not he/she). ✅

### Rule 3 — Reflexive pronoun
> He hurt **himself**. ✅
> He hurt **him**. ❌ (would mean someone else)

## 🔴 PREPOSITIONS (commonly tested pairs)

| Correct | Wrong |
|---------|-------|
| Married **to** | married **with** |
| Die **of** a disease | die **from** ✓ also acceptable |
| Afraid **of** | afraid **from** |
| Different **from** | different **than** (British: different **to**) |
| Consist **of** | consist **with** |
| Conform **to** | conform **with** |
| Cope **with** | cope **against** |
| Comply **with** | comply **to** |
| Angry **with** a person | angry **at** a thing |
| Compare **with** (similar things) | compare **to** (dissimilar things) |

## 🟠 SOLVED EXAMPLES — ERROR SPOTTING

### Example 1
> "The number of students in the class (A) are (B) increasing (C) every year (D)."

"The number of" → singular verb
**Error at B**: "are" should be "is"

### Example 2
> "Each of the students (A) were asked (B) to submit (C) their assignment (D)."

"Each of" → singular → "were" should be "was"
**Error at B**

### Example 3
> "Neither of the two boys (A) have (B) completed (C) their homework (D)."

"Neither of" → singular → "have" should be "has"
**Error at B**

### Example 4
> "If I was (A) you, I would (B) not have (C) made that mistake (D)."

Subjunctive mood: "If I were you" (hypothetical)
**Error at A**: "was" should be "were"

### Example 5
> "He suggested (A) me to apply (B) for the position (C) as soon as possible (D)."

"Suggest" is not followed by object + infinitive in formal English.
Correct: "He suggested that I apply" or "He advised me to apply"
**Error at A–B boundary**

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES

- "A number of" vs "The number of" — opposite verb agreement
- "One of the X who" — verb agrees with X, not "one"
- Articles before vowel sounds, not just vowel letters ("a university", "an hour")
- Tense: using present perfect with "ago" (wrong — use simple past)
- "Suggest" — cannot be followed by object + infinitive
