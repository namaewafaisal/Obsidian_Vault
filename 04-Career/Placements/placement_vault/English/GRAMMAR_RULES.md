# ⚡ GRAMMAR QUICK REFERENCE

---

## SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT
- Each / Every / Either / Neither / None → **singular verb**
- A number of → **plural** | The number of → **singular**
- One of the X who → verb agrees with **X** (plural)
- Or/Nor → verb agrees with **nearest subject**
- Data / News / Information / Furniture → **singular**
- Collective noun (team, committee) → usually **singular**
- Two subjects joined by "and" → **plural** (unless one concept: "bread and butter is...")

## TENSES
- have/has + past participle = Present Perfect (recent/unspecified past)
- had + past participle = Past Perfect (earlier of two past events)
- Since → Present Perfect | Ago → Simple Past (not both together)
- If I **were** you (subjunctive — hypothetical) NOT "was"
- Would/Could/Should/Might + base verb (not infinitive)

## ARTICLES
- a = before consonant SOUND | an = before vowel SOUND
- a university ✅ (yoo sound) | an hour ✅ (silent h)
- The = specific/known/unique/superlative
- No article: general plural nouns, languages, sports, meals, titles+name

## PRONOUNS
- After prepositions: me/him/her/us/them (NOT I/he/she/we/they)
- Between you and **me** ✅ (not I)
- Reflexive: himself/herself/themselves (cannot replace object pronoun)

## COMMON PREPOSITIONS
- since (point) vs for (duration)
- married **to** | afraid **of** | different **from** | consist **of**
- angry **with** person | angry **at** thing
- good **at** | interested **in** | fond **of**

## DEGREES OF COMPARISON
- Never double: more better ❌ → better ✅ | most tallest ❌ → tallest ✅
- Preferable (already comparative) → not "more preferable"
- "Senior/Junior/Prior/Superior/Inferior" → use "to" not "than"
  - Senior **to** (not senior than) ✅

## REPORTED SPEECH (Indirect Speech)
- "I am tired" → He said he **was** tired. (tense shifts back)
- "I will go" → He said he **would** go.
- Direct question: "Are you ready?" → He asked if I **was** ready.

## VOICE (Active/Passive)
- Active: Subject does the action
- Passive: Subject receives the action → Object + is/was/were + past participle + by + subject
- "By whom was the letter written?" not "By who"

## COMMON ERRORS TABLE
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| He has went | He has gone |
| The datas are | The data are/is |
| More preferable | Preferable |
| Between you and I | Between you and me |
| She suggested me to go | She suggested that I go |
| I have met him yesterday | I met him yesterday (simple past with "yesterday") |
| A large number of people was | A large number of people were |
| He plays the cricket | He plays cricket (no article for sports) |

---

## SINCE vs FOR
- Since = starting point: since Monday, since 2010, since childhood
- For = duration: for 3 years, for a long time, for two days

put **off** = postpone | look **into** = investigate | give **up** = stop
break **down** = fail | take **over** = assume control | pass **off** = pretend
