## Why?

You answered:

```text
She quickly ran.
```

But:
`quickly` modifies only verb `ran`.

That is a normal adverb.

---

## Sentence Adverb

```text
Honestly, I disagree.
```

`Honestly` comments on entire sentence/opinion.

Other examples:

```text
Fortunately, nobody was hurt.
Sadly, he left.
Clearly, you understand.
```
