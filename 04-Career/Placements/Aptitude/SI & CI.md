# 💰 Simple & Compound Interest — Complete Formula Sheet

---

## 🔴 SIMPLE INTEREST (SI)

### When to use SI
- Interest calculated on **original principal only**
- Words like: *simple interest*, *flat*, *per year (simple)*

---

### Simple Interest Formula
$$
SI = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}
$$

Where:
- P = Principal  
- R = Rate % per annum  
- T = Time (years)

---

### Amount in SI
$$
A = P + SI
$$

---

### Finding Principal
$$
P = \frac{SI \times 100}{R \times T}
$$

---

### Finding Rate
$$
R = \frac{SI \times 100}{P \times T}
$$

---

### Finding Time
$$
T = \frac{SI \times 100}{P \times R}
$$

---

## 🔴 COMPOUND INTEREST (CI)

### When to use CI
- Interest added back to principal
- Words like: *compound*, *growth*, *depreciation*, *population*, *value increases yearly*

---

### Amount Formula (Annual Compounding)
$$
A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T
$$

---

### Compound Interest
$$
CI = A - P
$$

---

## 🟠 CI WITH DIFFERENT COMPOUNDING PERIODS

### Half-Yearly
$$
A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{200}\right)^{2T}
$$

---

### Quarterly
$$
A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{400}\right)^{4T}
$$

---

## 🟠 CI VS SI (VERY IMPORTANT)

### Difference between CI and SI for 2 Years
$$
CI - SI = P \times \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2
$$

---

### Difference between CI and SI for 3 Years
$$
CI - SI = P \times \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2 \times \left(3 + \frac{R}{100}\right)
$$

---

## 🟡 POPULATION / GROWTH / DEPRECIATION

### Growth
$$
A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T
$$

---

### Depreciation
$$
A = P \times \left(1 - \frac{R}{100}\right)^T
$$

---

## 🟡 SUCCESSIVE RATE CHANGE (YEARLY)

If rate changes every year:
$$
A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R_1}{100}\right)
\times \left(1 + \frac{R_2}{100}\right)
$$

---

## 🟢 QUICK USE RULES (WRITE AS NOTES)

- SI → linear growth  
- CI → exponential growth  
- Population, value, depreciation → always CI  
- If **difference between CI & SI asked** → use direct formula (don’t compute fully)  
- If compounding period given → adjust rate & time
