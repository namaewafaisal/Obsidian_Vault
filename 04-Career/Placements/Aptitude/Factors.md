# 🔢 Number System — Factors, Squares & Cubes

---

## 🔴 PRIME FACTOR FORM

If:
$$
N = p^a q^b r^c
$$

---

## 🔴 NUMBER OF FACTORS

$$
\text{Total factors} = (a+1)(b+1)(c+1)
$$

---

## 🔴 PERFECT SQUARE RULE

- A number is a **perfect square** ⇔  
  **all prime powers are even**
- Perfect square ⇒ **odd number of factors**

---

## 🔴 PERFECT CUBE RULE

- A number is a **perfect cube** ⇔  
  **all prime powers are multiples of 3**

---

## 🟠 ODD & EVEN FACTORS

If:
$$
N = 2^a \times (\text{odd part})
$$

- **Odd factors** = factors of odd part
- **Even factors** = Total − Odd

---

## 🟠 SMALLEST NUMBER TO MULTIPLY

To make N:
- perfect square → make all powers even  
- perfect cube → make all powers multiple of 3  

(Multiply missing prime powers)

---

## 🟡 QUICK NOTES

- Only perfect squares have odd factors
- Check powers, not values
- Used heavily in MCQs
