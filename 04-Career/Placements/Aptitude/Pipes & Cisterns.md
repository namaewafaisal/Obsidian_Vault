# 🚰 Pipes & Cistern — Aptitude Master Notes (Exam-Oriented)

> **Scope:** Placement aptitude  
> **Goal:** Treat pipes exactly like Time & Work with signs

---

## 1. Core Concept

Pipes problems are **Time & Work problems** with signs.

- Inlet pipe → **adds work (+)**
- Outlet pipe → **removes work (−)**

---

## 2. One Pipe

If a pipe fills a tank in x hours:
$$
\text{1-hour work} = \frac{1}{x}
$$

If a pipe empties a tank in y hours:
$$
\text{1-hour work} = -\frac{1}{y}
$$

---

## 3. Multiple Pipes Together

Net work per hour:
$$
\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} - \frac{1}{z}
$$

Time to fill tank:
$$
\frac{1}{\text{Net work}}
$$

---

## 4. LCM (Unit Work) Method

### When to Use
- More than two pipes
- Fractional answers

### Method
1. Take LCM of filling/emptying times
2. Assign +ve and −ve work units
3. Find net work per hour
4. Divide total work by net rate

---

## 5. Alternate Filling & Emptying

If:
- Pipe A fills in a hours
- Pipe B empties in b hours
- Pipes open alternately

Solve hour by hour using:
$$
\text{Net work per cycle}
$$

---

## 6. Leak Problems

Treat leak as **outlet pipe**.

If:
- Tank fills in x hours without leak
- Tank fills in y hours with leak

Then leak empties tank in:
$$
\frac{1}{y} - \frac{1}{x}
$$

---

## 7. Partial Filling Problems

Steps:
1. Calculate work done till pipe is closed/opened
2. Subtract from total work
3. Divide remaining work by remaining pipe rate

---

## 8. Common Exam Traps

- Forgetting negative sign for outlet
- Adding times instead of rates
- Ignoring partial work
- Mixing hours and minutes

---

## Final Takeaways

- Pipes = Time & Work with signs
- Always work in rates
- Use LCM if confused
- Inlet (+), Outlet (−)
