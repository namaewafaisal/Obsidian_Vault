# 🔢 Number System — Simplification, Surds & Indices

---

## 🔴 LAWS OF INDICES (MUST WRITE)

$$
a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}
$$

$$
\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}
$$

$$
(a^m)^n = a^{mn}
$$

---

## 🔴 FRACTIONAL POWERS

$$
a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}
$$

$$
a^{m/n} = (\sqrt[n]{a})^m
$$

---

## 🔴 SURDS

$$
\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}
$$

$$
\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}
$$

---

## 🟠 RATIONALIZATION

If denominator has a surd:
- Multiply numerator and denominator by its conjugate

---

## 🟡 QUICK NOTES

- Convert everything to same base
- Prefer powers over roots
- Used in simplification & MCQs
