# 🇯🇵 Hiragana — Complete Foundation

Hiragana is a **phonetic script** consisting of **46 basic characters**.  
It is used for **native Japanese words**, **grammatical particles**, and **verb/adjective endings**.  
Mastery of Hiragana is the **first non-negotiable step** toward Japanese literacy.

---

## 🔤 Basic Hiragana (Monographs)

Each character represents **one syllable**, usually a consonant + vowel.  
Japanese has **five stable vowels**:

- **a** (ah)
- **i** (ee)
- **u** (oo, unrounded)
- **e** (eh)
- **o** (oh)

| Consonant  | a      | i       | u       | e      | o          |
| ---------- | ------ | ------- | ------- | ------ | ---------- |
| **Vowels** | あ (a)  | い (i)   | う (u)   | え (e)  | お (o)      |
| **K**      | か (ka) | き (ki)  | く (ku)  | け (ke) | こ (ko)     |
| **S**      | さ (sa) | し (shi) | す (su)  | せ (se) | そ (so)     |
| **T**      | た (ta) | ち (chi) | つ (tsu) | て (te) | と (to)     |

| **H**      | は (ha) | ひ (hi)  | ふ (fu)  | へ (he) | ほ (ho)     |

## 🔊 Voiced & Plosive Sounds (Diacritics)

Adding marks changes pronunciation:

- **Dakuten (゛)** → voiced
- **Handakuten (゜)** → plosive

### Changes
- **K → G:** が ぎ ぐ げ ご  
- **S → Z:** ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ  
- **T → D:** だ ぢ づ で ど  
- **H → B:** ば び ぶ べ ぼ  
- **H → P:** ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ

## 🔗 Combination Sounds (Digraphs)

Formed by an **i-row kana + small や / ゆ / よ**.

### Common Digraphs
- **kya, kyu, kyo:** きゃ きゅ きょ  
- **sha, shu, sho:** しゃ しゅ しょ  
- **cha, chu, cho:** ちゃ ちゅ ちょ  
- **nya, nyu, nyo:** にゃ にゅ にょ  
- **hya, hyu, hyo:** ひゃ ひゅ ひょ  
- **mya, myu, myo:** みゃ みゅ みょ  
- **rya, ryu, ryo:** りゃ りゅ りょ  
- **gya, gyu, gyo:** ぎゃ ぎゅ ぎょ  
- **ja, ju, jo:** じゃ じゅ じょ  
- **bya, byu, byo:** びゃ びゅ びょ  
- **pya, pyu, pyo:** ぴゃ ぴゅ ぴょ

## 🗣 Pronunciation Notes (Critical)

### Vowels
- **a:** father  
- **i:** meet  
- **u:** mood (less rounded lips)  
- **e:** net  
- **o:** so  

### Consonants
- **R (ら行):** Light tap (Spanish *r* / “dd” in *Eddy*)
- **F (ふ):** Soft airflow between lips (not teeth)
- **TSU (つ):** Like *ts* in *cats*

### Devoicing
- **i / u** may be whispered between voiceless consonants  
  - です → “dess”  
  - ます → “mahss”

---

## ⚠️ Special Particle Pronunciations

When used as particles:
- **は** → *wa*  
- **へ** → *e*  
- **を** → *o*  

---

## ✨ Special Symbols

### Small っ (Sokuon)
- Indicates a **double consonant**
- Creates a brief pause  
- Example: かった (*katta*)

### Long Vowels
- Written with extra vowels  
- Must be held **twice as long**
- Example: おかあさん (*okaasan*)
