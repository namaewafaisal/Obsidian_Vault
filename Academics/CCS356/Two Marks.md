# 1. What are Functional Requirements?

Functional requirements describe what the system should do. They define the main operations and services provided by the software. For example, login, registration, generating reports, or calculating salary are functional requirements. They are directly visible to the user.

---

# 2. Write the IEEE definition of Software Engineering.

According to IEEE, Software Engineering is the application of a systematic, disciplined, and measurable approach to the development, operation, and maintenance of software. It ensures quality and reliability in software development.

---

# 3. What is Software? List the characteristics.

Software is a collection of programs, procedures, and documentation that instruct a computer to perform tasks.

Characteristics:
- It is developed, not manufactured.
- It does not wear out like hardware.
- It can be modified.
- It is intangible.
- It increases in complexity over time.

# 5. List two deficiencies in Waterfall Model.

1. Changes cannot be easily incorporated once development starts.  
2. It is not suitable for complex and long-term projects.

---

# 6. Why must SRS be traceable? What is traceability requirement?

SRS must be traceable so that each requirement can be tracked from its origin to implementation and testing. Traceability ensures that no requirement is missed and helps in change management.

---

# 7. What is the outcome of Feasibility Study?

The outcome of a feasibility study is a feasibility report. It determines whether the project is technically, economically, legally, and operationally possible.

---

# 8. List out Requirements Engineering activities.

Requirements Engineering activities include:
1. Requirement Elicitation  
2. Requirement Analysis  
3. Requirement Specification  
4. Requirement Validation  
5. Requirement Management  

---

# 9. Define UML.

UML (Unified Modeling Language) is a standardized modeling language used to represent the design and structure of software systems using diagrams.

---

# 10. When to use Use Case Diagram?

Use Case Diagram is used during requirement gathering to show interactions between users (actors) and the system. It helps define system scope and functionality.

---

# 11. What is Software Engineering?

Software Engineering is the disciplined approach to designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software systems to ensure quality and efficiency.

---

# 12. State few differences between Traditional and Agile Software Development.

Traditional Model:
- Sequential process
- Heavy documentation
- Less flexible to change

Agile Model:
- Iterative process
- Continuous customer feedback
- Flexible and adaptive to changes

---

# 13. What are merits of Waterfall Model?

- Easy to understand and implement.
- Clear documentation.
- Fixed phases and milestones.
- Suitable for small projects with fixed requirements.

---

# 14. Give any two Software Engineering Challenges.

1. Managing changing requirements.  
2. Delivering software within time and budget.

Other challenges include maintaining quality and handling system complexity.

# 16. Write distinct steps in Requirement Engineering Process.

1. Elicitation – Gathering requirements.  
2. Analysis – Studying and refining requirements.  
3. Specification – Documenting requirements in SRS.  
4. Validation – Checking correctness of requirements.  
5. Management – Handling changes in requirements.

---

# 17. What are Non-Functional Requirements?

Non-functional requirements define quality attributes of a system such as performance, reliability, security, usability, and scalability. They describe how the system performs its tasks.

---

# 18. What is the need for Feasibility Analysis?

Feasibility analysis helps determine whether the proposed project is technically possible, economically viable, legally acceptable, and achievable within schedule.

---

# 19. List the characteristics of good SRS.

A good SRS should be:
- Clear
- Complete
- Consistent
- Unambiguous
- Verifiable
- Traceable
- Feasible

---

# 20. What is the purpose of Petri Net?

Petri Net is used to model systems that involve parallel or concurrent processes. It helps analyze synchronization, resource sharing, and process flow in complex systems.
