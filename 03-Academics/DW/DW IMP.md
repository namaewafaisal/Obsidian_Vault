Here is the organized data warehousing syllabus question bank categorized into **PART A (Short Answers)** and **PART B & C (Long Answers)**.

The questions are sorted from **highest frequency to lowest frequency**. Where frequencies are identical, the topics are ordered sequentially by **UNIT (Unit 1 to Unit 5)**.

## PART B & C: LONG ANSWER QUESTIONS

### Frequency: 8 Times

- **Unit 4 (Schemas & Modeling):** Deep dive into schemas—Design or propose Star, Snowflake, or Fact Constellation schemas; explain Snowflake normalization and layers; compare Star vs. Snowflake.
    

### Frequency: 6 Times

- **Unit 1:** Explain the Three-Tier Data Warehouse Architecture, its sketch, and its significance.
    

### Frequency: 5 Times

- **Unit 1:** Discuss in detail the concept and features of an Autonomous Data Warehouse (or compare it with Snowflake).
    
- **Unit 3:** Explain the role/importance of a Metadata Repository and the unique challenges associated with metadata management.
    

### Frequency: 4 Times

- **Unit 3:** Discuss the ETL process in data warehousing, its key steps, and how it differs from ELT.
    
- **Unit 4:** Describe the process of designing a cost-effective data mart and explain why it is needed for specific business scenarios.
    

### Frequency: 3 Times

- **Unit 2:** Explain the general characteristics of OLAP along with its typical analytical operations.
    
- **Unit 2:** Explain the concept of a Data Cube and its exact role in multi-dimensional data modeling (including sample design queries).
    
- **Unit 5:** Explain the operational relationship between the Load Manager and the Warehouse Manager in processing/maintenance.
    

### Frequency: 2 Times

- **Unit 2:** Compare OLAP systems directly with OLTP systems for data analysis.
    
- **Unit 2:** Diagrammatically illustrate and describe the architectures of MOLAP, ROLAP, and HOLAP (and highlight their core differences).
    
- **Unit 2:** Explain the phases of the delivery process in a data warehouse environment.
    
- **Unit 3:** Compare and contrast the advantages and disadvantages of vertical partitioning versus horizontal partitioning.
    
- **Unit 4:** Detail the process of dimensional modeling and the distinct advantages of using a multi-dimensional data model.
    
- **Unit 4:** Discuss the various types of database parallelism and data warehouse tools.
    
- **Unit 5:** Explain the concept of data warehouse tuning and testing process types that contribute to quality assurance.
    
- **Unit 5:** Explain the roles, functions, and cross-collaboration of various system and process managers.
    
- **Unit 5:** Discuss the specific functions of the System Configuration Manager, Scheduling Manager, and Event Manager.
    

### Frequency: 1 Time

- **Unit 1:** Compare and contrast an Operational Database with a Data Warehouse in a long-form response.
    
- **Unit 1:** Explain the general classification types of data warehouses in detail.
    
- **Unit 1:** Describe the technologies and alternative approaches used to improve overall performance in a data warehouse environment.
    
- **Unit 2:** Discuss typical OLAP operations specifically mapping onto an example of Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD).
