### 1. Define a Smart City and its key characteristics.

**Smart City:**
A city that uses Information and Communication Technology (ICT) and IoT technologies to improve the quality of life of citizens, optimize resources, and provide efficient services.

**Key Characteristics:**

* Smart governance
* Smart transportation
* Smart energy management
* Smart healthcare
* Smart environment
* Smart infrastructure
* Efficient resource utilization
* Enhanced public safety

### 2. What are the design metrics for embedded systems?

**Design Metrics** are parameters used to evaluate the quality and performance of an embedded system.

**Common Design Metrics:**

* Performance (speed of execution)
* Cost
* Power consumption
* Size and memory requirements
* Reliability
* Flexibility
* Maintainability
* Time-to-market

### 3. Explain the concept of smart irrigation.

**Smart Irrigation** is an IoT-based irrigation system that automatically controls water supply to crops based on soil and environmental conditions.

**Working:**

* Soil moisture sensors measure moisture levels.
* The controller analyzes sensor data.
* Water pumps/valves are automatically turned ON or OFF as needed.

**Benefits:**

* Saves water
* Reduces human effort
* Improves crop yield
* Prevents overwatering and underwatering

**Example:** If the soil moisture falls below a predefined level, the system automatically starts irrigation and stops it when sufficient moisture is reached.
