### 1. Define ZigBee and its typical application range.

**ZigBee** is a low-power, low-data-rate wireless communication protocol based on IEEE 802.15.4, used for short-range communication between IoT devices.

**Typical Application Range:**

* About **10–100 meters**, depending on the environment and transmission power.

**Applications:**

* Home automation
* Smart lighting
* Industrial monitoring
* Wireless sensor networks

### 2. What are the states involved in a Bluetooth connection?

The main Bluetooth connection states are:

1. **Standby** – Device is idle and not connected.
2. **Inquiry** – Device searches for other Bluetooth devices.
3. **Page** – Establishes a connection with a discovered device.
4. **Connected** – Data communication takes place between devices.

---

### 3. Mention the role of the GSM/GPRS module in IoT.

A **GSM/GPRS module** enables IoT devices to communicate over cellular networks.

**Roles:**

* Sending and receiving data over the internet (GPRS)
* Sending SMS alerts and notifications
* Making voice calls if required
* Providing remote monitoring and control where Wi-Fi is unavailable

**Examples:** Vehicle tracking systems, remote agriculture monitoring, smart energy meters.
