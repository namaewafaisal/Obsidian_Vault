## Group 1 – Cloud Multimedia and Streaming

> Big Question:
>
> How is multimedia stored and delivered to millions of users?

### Multimedia Cloud Computing

Multimedia cloud computing uses cloud infrastructure to store, process, and deliver multimedia content.

Services provided:

* Storage
* Processing
* Transcoding
* Streaming

Examples:

* Netflix
* YouTube
* Google Photos

---

### Advantages

* Scalability
* Cost efficiency
* High availability
* Anywhere access

---

### Media-on-Demand (MoD)

Users access multimedia content whenever needed.

Examples:

* Netflix
* Spotify
* Amazon Prime Video

Features:

* User-controlled playback
* Personalized recommendations

---

### Multimedia Streaming

Streaming delivers audio and video continuously without downloading the entire file.

Types:

* Live streaming
* On-demand streaming

Examples:

* YouTube Live
* Twitch

---

## Group 2 – Multimedia Analytics and Surveillance

> Big Question:
>
> How is multimedia data analyzed?

### Multimedia Analytics

Multimedia analytics extracts useful information from multimedia data.

Data sources:

* Images
* Videos
* Audio
* Social media

Applications:

* Recommendation systems
* Sentiment analysis
* Traffic monitoring

---

### Surveillance Multimedia

Uses cameras and sensors to monitor activities.

Examples:

* CCTV systems
* Smart cities
* Traffic management

Technologies:

* Face recognition
* Object detection
* Motion tracking

---

## Group 3 – Content-Based Retrieval and Ontology

> Big Question:
>
> How do systems understand multimedia content?

### Content-Based Retrieval (CBR)

Retrieves multimedia using content features instead of keywords.

Features used:

* Color
* Shape
* Texture
* Audio patterns

Example:

* Reverse image search

---

### Multimedia Ontology

Ontology defines relationships between multimedia concepts.

Example:

```text id="ontology"
Vehicle
   ↓
Car
   ↓
Sports Car
```

Purpose:

* Better search
* Improved classification
* Semantic understanding

---

## Group 4 – Security and Digital Forensics

> Big Question:
>
> How is multimedia protected and investigated?

### Multimedia Security

Protects multimedia content from unauthorized access.

Techniques:

* Encryption
* Watermarking
* Authentication
* Access control

Applications:

* Copyright protection
* Secure streaming

---

### Digital Forensics

Digital forensics involves collecting and analyzing digital evidence.

Tasks:

* Recover deleted files
* Verify authenticity
* Analyze metadata

Applications:

* Cybercrime investigation
* Legal evidence

---
