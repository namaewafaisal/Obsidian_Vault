# 3.5 — Scrum

An Agile framework for managing team workflow.

Focus:
- sprint-based development
- team coordination
- iterative delivery

---

# Scrum Roles

## Product Owner
- manages backlog
- prioritizes features

## Scrum Master
- ensures Scrum workflow
- removes blockers

## Development Team
- builds the product

---

# Scrum Artifacts

## Product Backlog
Complete prioritized task list.

## Sprint Backlog
Tasks selected for current sprint.

## Increment
Working software after sprint.

---

# Scrum Events

## Sprint
Short iteration:
- usually 2–4 weeks

## Sprint Planning
Select sprint tasks.

## Daily Standup
Short daily progress meeting.

## Sprint Review
Demonstrate completed work.

## Sprint Retrospective
Reflect and improve workflow.

---

# Velocity

Amount of work completed in a sprint.

Used for:
- estimation
- sprint planning

---

# Scrum vs XP

| Scrum | XP |
|---|---|
| Workflow framework | Engineering practices |
| Sprint management | TDD, CI, Pair Programming |
| Team coordination | Technical discipline |

---

# Important Insight

Scrum manages:
> how teams organize work

XP manages:
> how developers build software
