# 4.2 — Requirements Elicitation Techniques

Elicitation:
> extracting requirements from stakeholders.

---

# Techniques

## Interviews
Direct stakeholder discussion.

Types:
- structured
- unstructured

---

## Observation (Ethnography)
Observe users doing real work.

Used to discover:
- implicit requirements
- workflow issues

---

## Prototyping
Early simplified system for feedback.

### Types
- Throwaway Prototype
- Evolutionary Prototype

---

## JAD (Joint Application Development)
Workshop involving:
- developers
- customers
- stakeholders

Used for collaborative requirement gathering.

---

## Questionnaires
Used for:
- large user groups
- scalable feedback collection

---

# Technique Comparison

| Technique | Best For |
|---|---|
| Interview | detailed discussion |
| Observation | real workflow discovery |
| Prototyping | unclear requirements |
| JAD | stakeholder collaboration |
| Questionnaire | large user groups |

---

# Important Insight

Users often cannot clearly explain:
> what they actually need.

Multiple elicitation techniques are usually combined.
