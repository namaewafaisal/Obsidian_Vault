# 8.4 — Risk Management

Risk Management:
> identifying and handling possible project problems before they occur.

Goal:
> reduce impact of uncertainties on the project.

---

# What Is a Risk?

A risk is:
> a potential future problem that may affect:
- schedule
- budget
- quality
- delivery

Examples:

```text
- key developer leaving
- API failure
- requirement changes
- server outage
```

---

# Main Risk Management Activities

| Activity | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Risk Identification | Find possible risks |
| Risk Analysis | Estimate probability/impact |
| Risk Prioritization | Focus on important risks |
| Risk Mitigation | Reduce risk impact |
| Risk Monitoring | Track risks continuously |

---

# Risk Analysis

Evaluates:
- probability of occurrence
- impact severity

Example:

| Risk | Probability | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| UI bug | High | Low |
| Database corruption | Low | Very High |

---

# Risk Mitigation

Actions taken to reduce risk impact.

Example:

```text
Risk:
Payment gateway failure

Mitigation:
Prepare backup provider
```

---

# Types of Risks

| Risk Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Technical Risk | Technology failure |
| Schedule Risk | Development delays |
| Cost Risk | Budget overrun |
| Resource Risk | Team member leaving |
| Requirement Risk | Frequent requirement changes |
| Security Risk | Vulnerabilities/data leaks |

---

# Risk Exposure

```text
Risk Exposure =
Probability × Impact
```

Higher exposure:
> higher priority

---

# Contingency Plan

Backup plan prepared if risk occurs.

Example:

```text
Primary server fails
→ switch to backup server
```

---

# Reactive vs Proactive

| Reactive | Proactive |
|---|---|
| Handle after occurrence | Prepare before occurrence |
| Higher damage | Lower damage |

Good projects prefer:
> proactive risk management

---

# Important Insight

Risk Management helps:
> anticipate and reduce project problems before they become failures
