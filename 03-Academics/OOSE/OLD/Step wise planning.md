# 🧠 Stepwise Planning

## 🧩 Core Idea
Stepwise Planning =  
> **Plan a project in small, logical steps instead of all at once**

---

## ⚙️ Essential Steps

### 🔹 1. Define Objectives
- Identify project goal  

---

### 🔹 2. Define Scope
- What is included / excluded  

---

### 🔹 3. Identify Tasks
- Break project into smaller tasks  

---

### 🔹 4. Estimate Effort
- Time and work required  

---

### 🔹 5. Identify Dependencies
- Order of tasks  

---

### 🔹 6. Allocate Resources
- Assign people/tools  

---

### 🔹 7. Create Schedule
- Timeline for tasks  

---

### 🔹 8. Risk Analysis
- Identify possible risks  

---

### 🔹 9. Review & Adjust
- Refine the plan  

---

## 🔁 Workflow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Objectives] --> B[Scope]
    B --> C[Tasks]
    C --> D[Effort]
    D --> E[Dependencies]
    E --> F[Resources]
    F --> G[Schedule]
    G --> H[Risk Analysis]
    H --> I[Review]
```

---

## 📌 Importance
- Structured planning  
- Better control  
- Reduced risks  
- Efficient execution  

---

## 🧠 Final Understanding

Stepwise Planning =  
> **Systematic approach to plan projects step-by-step for better management**
