# 🧠 Analysing Project Characteristics

## 🧩 Core Idea
> Understanding the nature of a project to plan and manage it effectively

---

## ⚙️ Activities

### 🔹 Identify Project Scope
- Define features and boundaries  

---

### 🔹 Determine Project Size
- Small / Medium / Large  
- Based on modules or effort  

---

### 🔹 Identify Complexity
- Level of difficulty  
- Interactions between components  

---

### 🔹 Identify Technology Requirements
- Tools, frameworks, platforms  

---

### 🔹 Identify Resource Requirements
- Developers, tools, hardware  

---

### 🔹 Determine Time Constraints
- Deadlines and schedule  

---

### 🔹 Identify Risks
- Possible failures or delays  

---

### 🔹 Identify Dependencies
- Task relationships  

---

### 🔹 Choose Development Approach
- Agile, Waterfall, etc.  

---

## 🔁 Workflow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Scope] --> B[Size]
    B --> C[Complexity]
    C --> D[Technology]
    D --> E[Resources]
    E --> F[Time]
    F --> G[Risk]
    G --> H[Dependencies]
    H --> I[Approach]
```

---

## 📌 Importance
- Improves planning  
- Reduces risks  
- Optimizes resource usage  

---

## 🧠 Final Understanding

Project Characteristics Analysis =  
> **Evaluate project factors to guide planning and execution**
