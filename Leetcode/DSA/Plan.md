## Completion Rule (Strict)

A data structure is considered **complete** only when:
- Its **core invariant** is clear
- You know **what it is better/worse than**
- You can choose it **without guessing**

No algorithms are learned until the relevant structures are understood.

---

## One-Line Recall

> Learn data structures **in dependency order**, lock their invariants first, and only then layer algorithms on top.
