## 1. What prepositions test

Prepositions test:
- usage familiarity
- NOT logic

There is **no formula**.
Only pattern recognition.

---

## 2. Time Prepositions (VERY COMMON)

### at → exact time
- at 5 PM
- at night

### on → day/date
- on Monday
- on 5th Feb

### in → longer period
- in 2026
- in the morning

---

## 3. Place Prepositions

### at → point
- at the door
- at the station

### in → enclosed area
- in the room
- in India

### on → surface
- on the table
- on the wall

---

## 4. Common Exam Pairs (MEMORIZE)

- interested **in**
- good **at**
- depend **on**
- afraid **of**
- responsible **for**
- compare **with** / **to**

---

## 5. Preposition Rule (Exam mindset)

If it:
- “sounds right”
- and you’ve seen it before

👉 It’s probably correct.

Do NOT overthink.

---

## 6. One-line recall

> “Prepositions are learned by usage, not logic.”
