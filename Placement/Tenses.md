## 1. What tenses are tested for (exam reality)

Aptitude exams do NOT test:
- all 12 tenses
- complex transformations

They test:
- tense consistency
- basic time logic
- signal words

---

## 2. The ONLY tenses you must be solid in

### 1️⃣ Simple Present
Used for:
- facts
- habits
- universal truths

Example:
- Water **boils** at 100°C.

---

### 2️⃣ Simple Past
Used for:
- completed actions
- specific time in past

Signal words:
- yesterday
- last year
- ago

Example:
- He **completed** the task yesterday.

---

### 3️⃣ Present Continuous
Used for:
- action happening now
- temporary action

Signal words:
- now
- currently
- at present

Example:
- She **is working** now.

---

### 4️⃣ Present Perfect (IMPORTANT)

Used for:
- past action with present relevance
- unspecified time

Signal words:
- already
- yet
- just
- ever / never

Example:
- I **have finished** my work.

❌ Do NOT use with:
- yesterday
- last week
- in 2020

---

## 3. Past Perfect (exam-safe rule)

Used when:
- two past actions
- earlier action uses **past perfect**

Example:
- The train **had left** before I reached the station.

---

## 4. Tense Consistency Rule (VERY IMPORTANT)

Do NOT change tense unnecessarily.

Wrong:
- He **was going** to college and **meets** his friend.

Correct:
- He **was going** to college and **met** his friend.

---

## 5. Common Traps

❌ Mixing present perfect with past time  
❌ Switching tense mid-sentence  
❌ Using continuous for facts  

---

## 6. One-line recall

> “Tense must match time and remain consistent.”
