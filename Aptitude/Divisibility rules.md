# 🔢 Number System — Divisibility Rules (Formula Sheet)

---

## 🔴 CORE IDEA

A number is divisible by another number if it satisfies the **specific test** for that divisor.

---

## 🔴 BASIC DIVISIBILITY RULES (MUST MEMORIZE)

### Divisibility by 2
- Last digit is **even** (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)

---

### Divisibility by 3
- Sum of digits divisible by 3

---

### Divisibility by 4
- Last **two digits** divisible by 4

---

### Divisibility by 5
- Last digit is **0 or 5**

---

### Divisibility by 6
- Divisible by **2 and 3**

---

### Divisibility by 8
- Last **three digits** divisible by 8

---

### Divisibility by 9
- Sum of digits divisible by 9

---

### Divisibility by 10
- Last digit is **0**

---

### Divisibility by 11
- Difference between:
  - Sum of digits at **odd places**
  - Sum of digits at **even places**
- Result = 0 or multiple of 11

---

### Divisibility by 12
- Divisible by **3 and 4**

---

## 🟠 COMPOSITE DIVISIBILITY (IMPORTANT)

### Divisible by 18
- Divisible by **2 and 9**

### Divisible by 24
- Divisible by **3 and 8**

### Divisible by 36
- Divisible by **4 and 9**

---

## 🟡 QUICK NOTES

- Always test **smaller factors first**
- For large numbers → use digit rules
- Divisibility questions often hide inside:
  - “Find smallest number”
  - “Replace digit”
  - “How many values of x”

---

## ❌ DO NOT WRITE

- Proofs of rules  
- Long digit manipulation
