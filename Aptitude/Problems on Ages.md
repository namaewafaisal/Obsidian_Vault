# 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Problems on Ages — Complete Formula Sheet (Most → Least Important)

---

## 🔴 CORE RULES (MUST WRITE)

If present age = x

- After n years:
$$
x + n
$$

- n years ago:
$$
x - n
$$

---

## 🔴 RATIO METHOD (MOST USED)

If present age ratio = a : b  
Assume present ages:
$$
ax,\; bx
$$

---

## 🔴 GOLDEN RULE (VERY IMPORTANT)

**Difference of ages is constant**

If A and B are two persons:
$$
(A - B)_{\text{present}} = (A - B)_{\text{past}} = (A - B)_{\text{future}}
$$

---

## 🟠 WHEN RATIOS AT DIFFERENT TIMES ARE GIVEN

Steps:
1. Write ratios using variables  
2. Apply time shift ( +n or −n )  
3. Use constant difference rule  
4. Solve for x

---

## 🟠 AVERAGE AGE

Average:
$$
\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of ages}}{\text{Number of persons}}
$$

Sum of ages:
$$
\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Number of persons}
$$

---

## 🟠 ADD / REMOVE PERSON (SHORTCUT)

If one person is replaced:

Change in sum:
$$
\text{New Sum} - \text{Old Sum}
$$

Age of new person:
$$
\text{Old age} + (\text{Number of persons} \times \text{Change in average})
$$

---

## 🟡 FATHER–SON / MOTHER–CHILD

- Always maintain constant age difference
- Use ratio at one time → find actual ages
- Apply future / past condition

---

## 🟡 MULTIPLE PERSONS

If ages of n persons increase by k years each:
$$
\text{Increase in sum} = n \times k
$$

---

## 🟢 COMMON SHORTCUT NOTES

- Use ratio method first  
- Convert words → equations immediately  
- Avoid assuming actual ages early  
- Use difference rule to eliminate variables  

---

## ❌ DO NOT WRITE

- Trial-and-error tables  
- Guessing ages  
- Long story explanations
