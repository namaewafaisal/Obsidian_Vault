# 🔢 Number System — HCF & LCM (Formula Sheet)

---

## 🔴 DEFINITIONS (MUST WRITE)

- **HCF (GCD):** Greatest number dividing all exactly  
- **LCM:** Least number divisible by all numbers  

---

## 🔴 CORE FORMULA (VERY IMPORTANT)

For two numbers:
$$
\text{Product of numbers} = \text{HCF} \times \text{LCM}
$$

---

## 🔴 PRIME FACTOR METHOD

If:
$$
N = p^a q^b r^c
$$

- **HCF:** product of **lowest powers**
- **LCM:** product of **highest powers**

---

## 🟠 HCF & LCM OF FRACTIONS

### HCF
$$
\frac{\text{HCF of numerators}}{\text{LCM of denominators}}
$$

### LCM
$$
\frac{\text{LCM of numerators}}{\text{HCF of denominators}}
$$

---

## 🟠 WORD PROBLEM LOGIC

- “Smallest number divisible by …” → **LCM**
- “Largest number dividing …” → **HCF**
- “Leaves same remainder” → **LCM ± remainder**

---

## 🟡 QUICK NOTES

- If HCF × LCM is given → find missing number easily
- Reduce numbers before factorization
- Use LCM method for **time & work**, **pipes**, **numbers**

---

## ❌ DO NOT WRITE

- Division algorithm steps  
- Long factor trees
