# ⚡ Number System — High-Speed Shortcuts (Exam Use)

> Use ONLY after fundamentals are clear.

---

## 🔴 DIVISIBILITY SHORTCUTS

### Replace-Digit Questions
- For divisibility by **3 / 9** → check **digit sum**
- For divisibility by **11** →  
  (Odd place sum − Even place sum) = 0 or multiple of 11

---

### Last-Digits Check
- Divisible by 4 → last **2 digits**
- Divisible by 8 → last **3 digits**
- Ignore rest of the number

---

## 🔴 LCM + SAME REMAINDER (VERY IMPORTANT)

If a number leaves **same remainder r** when divided by:
- a, b, c

Then:
$$
\text{Number} = \text{LCM}(a,b,c) + r
$$

---

### Constant Difference (Gap Method)
If:
- Divided by a → remainder r₁  
- Divided by b → remainder r₂  

And:
$$
a - r_1 = b - r_2 = k
$$

Then:
$$
\text{Number} = \text{LCM}(a,b) - k
$$

---

## 🔴 UNIT DIGIT SHORTCUTS

### Power Ends in:
- 0,1,5,6 → unit digit **fixed**
- 4,9 → cycle of **2**
- 2,3,7,8 → cycle of **4**

---

### Multiple Terms
- Find unit digit of each term **separately**
- Add unit digits → final unit digit

---

## 🔴 REMAINDER SHORTCUTS

### Prime Modulo (Fermat)
If divisor = prime p:
$$
a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod p
$$

So reduce power using:
$$
\text{Exponent mod } (p-1)
$$

---

### −1 Trick
If:
$$
a \equiv -1 \pmod m
$$

Then:
- Even power → remainder 1
- Odd power → remainder −1

---

## 🔴 FACTOR SHORTCUTS

### Perfect Square
- All prime powers **even**
- Number of factors = **odd**

---

### Perfect Cube
- All prime powers **multiple of 3**

---

### Odd Factors
- Remove all powers of 2
- Count factors of remaining part

---

## 🔴 FACTORIAL SHORTCUTS

### Trailing Zeros
- Only count **powers of 5**
- Ignore powers of 2

---

### Unit Digit
- For n ≥ 5:
$$
\text{Unit digit of } n! = 0
$$

---

## 🔴 SIMPLIFICATION SHORTCUTS

- Convert roots → powers
- Convert everything → same base
- Cancel **before** calculating
- Never expand large powers

---

## 🟢 MCQ ELIMINATION TACTICS

- Options differ slightly → check **unit digit**
- Big numbers → use **modulo**
- Square/cube questions → check last digit rule first
- If answer must be integer → eliminate fractions early

---

## ❌ DO NOT USE SHORTCUTS WHEN

- Ratios or exact values are required
- Multiple conditions are mixed
- Question explicitly asks for method
