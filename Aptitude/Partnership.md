# 🤝 Partnership — Complete Formula Sheet (Most → Least Important)

---

## 🔴 CORE RULE (MUST WRITE)

### Profit Sharing Rule
$$
\text{Profit} \propto \text{Investment} \times \text{Time}
$$

---

## 🔴 PROFIT RATIO

If investments are \(A, B\) and times are \(T_1, T_2\):
$$
\text{Profit ratio} = A \times T_1 : B \times T_2
$$

---

## 🟠 EQUAL TIME CASE

If time is same:
$$
\text{Profit ratio} = \text{Investment ratio}
$$

---

## 🟠 EQUAL INVESTMENT CASE

If investment is same:
$$
\text{Profit ratio} = \text{Time ratio}
$$

---

## 🟠 CHANGE IN INVESTMENT / TIME

Steps:
1. Split time into periods  
2. Compute investment × time for each period  
3. Add contributions  

---

## 🟡 SLEEPING PARTNER

Sleeping partner:
- Shares profit  
- Does no work  
- Treated same in formula  

---

## 🟢 QUICK SHORTCUT NOTES

- Convert months → years if needed  
- Always multiply investment × time  
- Use LCM for different time periods
