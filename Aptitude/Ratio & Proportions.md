# ⚖️ Ratio & Proportion — Complete Formula Sheet (Most → Least Important)

---

## 🔴 CORE DEFINITIONS (MUST WRITE)

### Ratio
$$
a : b = \frac{a}{b}
$$

---

### Proportion
$$
a : b :: c : d \Rightarrow ad = bc
$$

---

## 🔴 RATIO SIMPLIFICATION

Multiply or divide both terms by same number.

---

## 🔴 DIVIDING A QUANTITY IN A GIVEN RATIO

If total = T and ratio = a : b:
$$
\text{First part} = \frac{a}{a+b} \times T
$$

$$
\text{Second part} = \frac{b}{a+b} \times T
$$

---

## 🟠 COMPOUND RATIO

If:
$$
a:b \text{ and } c:d
$$

Then:
$$
\text{Compound ratio} = ac : bd
$$

---

## 🟠 CONTINUED PROPORTION

If:
$$
a:b = b:c
$$

Then:
$$
b^2 = ac
$$

---

## 🟠 DIRECT & INVERSE PROPORTION

### Direct Proportion
$$
x \propto y \Rightarrow \frac{x_1}{x_2} = \frac{y_1}{y_2}
$$

### Inverse Proportion
$$
x \propto \frac{1}{y} \Rightarrow \frac{x_1}{x_2} = \frac{y_2}{y_1}
$$

---

## 🟡 VARIATION

If:
$$
x \propto y \Rightarrow x = ky
$$

If:
$$
x \propto \frac{1}{y} \Rightarrow x = \frac{k}{y}
$$
